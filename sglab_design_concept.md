# SGLab Website Design Concept

## Website Structure

### Navigation Menu
- **Home** - Landing page with hero section
- **About** - Lab overview and mission
- **Research** - Current projects and research areas
- **Publications** - Academic papers and talks
- **Team** - Current students and alumni
- **Contact** - Contact information and location

### Page Layout Design

#### 1. Home Page
- **Hero Section**: Large banner with lab team photo or AI/biology visualization
- **Welcome Message**: Brief introduction to SGLab
- **Research Highlights**: Key research areas with icons
- **Recent News**: Latest publications or achievements
- **Quick Links**: Direct access to important sections

#### 2. About Page
- **Lab Mission**: Research objectives and goals
- **Principal Investigator**: Dr. Javier E. Sanchez-Galan profile
- **Research Focus**: AI for Biology intersection
- **Lab History**: Establishment and evolution
- **Affiliations**: UTP, GIBBS, CEPIA, INDICASAT-AIP

#### 3. Research Page
- **Current Projects**: Active research initiatives
- **Research Areas**: 
  - Biomarker Discovery
  - Machine Learning in Spectral Analysis
  - Computational Biology
  - Smart Devices for m-health
- **Methodologies**: Technical approaches and tools
- **Collaborations**: Partner institutions

#### 4. Publications Page
- **Recent Papers**: Latest academic publications
- **Conference Presentations**: Talks and presentations
- **Thesis Supervision**: Student research projects
- **Media Coverage**: Press and outreach activities

#### 5. Team Page
- **Principal Investigator**: Dr. Sanchez-Galan
- **Current Students**: PhD, MSc, and undergraduate students
- **Alumni**: Former lab members and their current positions
- **Collaborators**: External research partners

#### 6. Contact Page
- **Contact Information**: Email, phone, address
- **Location**: UTP campus map
- **Office Hours**: Availability for meetings
- **Social Media**: Professional profiles

## Visual Design Concept

### Color Palette
- **Primary**: Deep blue (#1e3a8a) - representing technology and trust
- **Secondary**: Bright green (#10b981) - representing biology and growth
- **Accent**: Orange (#f59e0b) - representing innovation and energy
- **Neutral**: Gray tones (#6b7280, #f3f4f6) - for text and backgrounds
- **White**: (#ffffff) - for clean backgrounds

### Typography
- **Headers**: Modern sans-serif font (Inter or similar)
- **Body Text**: Clean, readable font (Open Sans or similar)
- **Code/Technical**: Monospace font (Fira Code or similar)

### Visual Elements
- **Logo**: User-provided geometric design with colorful blocks
- **Icons**: Modern, minimalist icons for research areas
- **Images**: High-quality photos of lab equipment, team, and research visualizations
- **Graphics**: Clean diagrams and infographics for research explanation

### Layout Style
- **Clean and Modern**: Inspired by the reference Leung Lab website
- **Responsive Design**: Mobile-first approach
- **Grid-based Layout**: Organized sections with proper spacing
- **Card-based Components**: For research projects and team members
- **Smooth Animations**: Subtle transitions and hover effects

## Technical Specifications

### Framework
- **Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript** for GitHub Pages compatibility
- **Bootstrap or Tailwind CSS** for responsive design
- **Font Awesome** for icons
- **Google Fonts** for typography

### Performance
- **Optimized Images**: Compressed and properly sized
- **Fast Loading**: Minimal JavaScript and CSS
- **SEO Friendly**: Proper meta tags and structure
- **Accessibility**: WCAG compliant design

### Responsive Breakpoints
- **Mobile**: 320px - 768px
- **Tablet**: 768px - 1024px
- **Desktop**: 1024px+
